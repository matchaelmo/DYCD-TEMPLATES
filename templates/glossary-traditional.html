Agency Reference

DYCD Glossary

Acronyms, abbreviations, and key terms used across the agency.

ACCO

Finance

Agency Chief Contracting Officer. The office responsible for all procurement and contract management at DYCD.

ACS

Programs

Administration for Children's Services. NYC agency that partners with DYCD on youth and family programs.

BEACON

Programs

A DYCD community center program providing after-school and evening programming in public schools.

CBO

Programs

Community-Based Organization. Nonprofit or community group that partners with DYCD to deliver programs.

CityTime

IT

NYC's web-based timekeeping system used by all city employees to log hours and manage leave.

COMPASS

IT

Citywide Online Monitoring, Planning and Reporting System. Used to track program data and outcomes.

DYCD

Agency

Department of Youth & Community Development. The NYC agency responsible for funding and overseeing youth and community programs.

EEO

HR

Equal Employment Opportunity. Policy and office ensuring fair treatment of all employees.

ESS

HR

Employee Self Service. The NYC portal for managing payroll, benefits, and personal HR information.

FMS

Finance

Financial Management System. The city's accounting and financial reporting platform.

GEIP

Agency

Government Efficiency & Innovation Program. Initiative focused on improving city government operations.

HR

HR

Human Resources. The division managing hiring, benefits, employee relations, and compliance.

IBO

Finance

Independent Budget Office. NYC agency that provides independent fiscal analysis.

LMS

HR

Learning Management System. Platform used for staff training, certifications, and professional development.

MOC

Finance

Mayor's Office of Contract Services. Oversees citywide procurement policies and vendor management.

NYCERS

HR

New York City Employees' Retirement System. Manages pension benefits for most city employees.

NTA

Legal

Notice to Appear. A legal document used in immigration proceedings, sometimes relevant to client services.

OTI

IT

Office of Technology & Innovation. The NYC office overseeing technology infrastructure and digital services citywide.

PASSPort

Finance

Procurement and Sourcing Solutions Portal. NYC's online system for managing contracts and vendors.

PEG

Finance

Program to Eliminate the Gap. A budget reduction initiative requiring agencies to identify savings.

RFP

Programs

Request for Proposals. A procurement document inviting vendors or CBOs to submit program proposals.

ServiceNow

IT

IT service management platform used by NYC agencies for help desk tickets and IT support.

SYEP

Programs

Summer Youth Employment Program. DYCD's flagship summer jobs program for NYC youth ages 14–24.

TDA

HR

Tax-Deferred Annuity. A retirement savings plan available to NYC employees through NYCERS.

WIA

Programs

Workforce Investment Act. Federal legislation funding workforce development programs.
